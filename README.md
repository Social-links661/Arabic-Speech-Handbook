## 🧭 What's Inside the Handbook?

The handbook is divided into logical sections that cover the full spectrum of Arabic speech technology. Here is a preview of what you will find:

### 1. 📊 Benchmarks and Leaderboards

This section gives you an overview of how different Arabic speech models perform. You will see comparisons, scores, and rankings that help you understand which systems are leading the field.

### 2. 🎧 ASR and General Speech Corpora

Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) is the technology that turns spoken words into text. This part lists the major datasets used to train and test these systems. You will learn about the size, language variety, and quality of each corpus.

### 3. 🗣️ TTS-Grade Corpora

Text-to-Speech (TTS) systems generate spoken audio from written text. This section focuses on datasets that are specifically suited for building natural-sounding Arabic voices.

### 4. 📚 Task-Specific Corpora and Adjacent Tasks

Arabic speech AI is not just about basic recognition. This part covers specialized areas such as dialect identification, emotion detection, speech translation, and pronunciation assessment. You will find resources for each of these advanced tasks.

### 5. 🛠️ Tooling and Production Engineering

Beyond the data, this section discusses the software tools, pipelines, and engineering practices needed to bring Arabic speech applications into the real world. It covers topics like model deployment, performance optimization, and quality assurance.
